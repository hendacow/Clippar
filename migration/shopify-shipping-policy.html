This policy covers the Clippar Mount Kit. It sits alongside the full Mount Kit Terms of Sale.

Where we ship

We ship within Australia only. That isn't a commercial preference — the kit contains a lithium-ion battery, which is classified as dangerous goods (UN3480) and can't be sent by air or exported without a compliant freight arrangement. We'd rather ship to fewer places properly than to more places badly.

Carrier and timeframes

Carrier and service: a road-only domestic service that accepts lithium batteries, with dangerous-goods labelling. We haven't signed the freight contract yet, so we're not going to name a carrier we might not use — we'll tell you which one it is, in writing, before your kit ships, and you can cancel for a full refund up to that point if you'd rather not accept it.

Dispatch: December 2026, from the first production run. This is a pre-order — see our Mount Kit Terms of Sale for the full pre-order terms, including your right to cancel any time before dispatch for a full refund.

Transit time: road freight is slower than air, so expect it to take longer than an Express Post parcel. We'll give you a realistic estimate with your tracking number rather than a number we've guessed at now.

You'll get a tracking number when the parcel is dispatched.

Delivery addresses

We can't deliver to PO boxes or parcel lockers for this product, because dangerous-goods freight needs a street address.

Please make sure your delivery address is right. If a parcel comes back to us because the address was wrong, we'll re-send it once you've paid the return and re-delivery freight.

If your delivery is late

Delivery estimates are estimates. If your parcel is materially late we'll chase the carrier and keep you posted.

If it's so late that it's no longer any use to you, tell us — under the consumer guarantees you may be entitled to a refund, and we won't argue about a delivery we got wrong. See our refund policy.

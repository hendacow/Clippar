## Flow summary

A single 8-screen conversion funnel that runs before any login wall, mounted as an expo-router group (app/(onboarding)/) and shown to anyone who lands cold from the Instagram reel. The arc is HOOK → MICRO-COMMIT → VALUE → PROOF → PAYWALL → TRIAL CONFIRM, matching the IG ad's exact line ("Every shot. Remembered.") for ad-to-app continuity. It opens on the real clippargolf.com demo_reel.mp4 autoplaying full-bleed so the golfer feels the payoff (tracer arc + scorecard + a made putt) before being asked anything. Two quick tap-chip questions (handicap, goal) turn passive viewers into invested ones via sunk-cost, and their answers are echoed verbatim into the value and paywall copy. Three value screens dramatize the three things this audience actually pays for: (1) raw 4hr footage collapsing into a 90-sec reel — the raw→detected→clean trio from the live site, (2) the signature gold tracer-arc draw that mirrors the in-app GPS shot tracer, animated on react-native-svg strokeDashoffset with a haptic at apex, and (3) the group-chat share moment. A "Building your highlight engine" Lottie loader manufactures earned value, then a scrollable proof wall `[ON HOLD — the rating, golfer count and reviews originally specified were fabricated]` pre-loads the "is it real / is it worth it" objections right before the ask. The paywall lands at peak motivation with a 7-day-free-trial timeline (Today → Day 5 reminder → Day 7 billed), annual pre-selected and framed as A$0.41/day with a gold SAVE 38% badge, an identity CTA ("Start filming my rounds"), and the value recap personalized to their answers. It converts because the price never appears cold — by the time it shows, the golfer has felt the dream, invested two taps, watched the magic, and (once real proof exists) seen that other golfers are already in. It is fully buildable today: every animation is reanimated 4 + lottie + react-native-svg + expo-haptics, every purchase call routes through the existing lib/iap.ts (annual/monthly ProPlan, priceLabel/badge), and the basic app/paywall.tsx is upgraded in place rather than replaced.

## Animation plan

All effects ship on libraries already in package.json — react-native-reanimated 4.1.1, lottie-react-native 7.3.6, react-native-svg 15.15.4, expo-haptics, expo-image, expo-video. NO new native modules, runs on the current dev build. By effect: (1) PAGE TRANSITIONS / ENTRANCES — reanimated withSpring/withTiming on opacity+translateY, one Haptics.selectionAsync() fired on each snap so transitions feel physical (every screen). (2) HERO + VALUE VIDEO — expo-video (loop, muted, autoplay, onReadyForDisplay→cross-fade) streaming the real clippargolf.com MP4s, with expo-image step posters as instant blurhash placeholders to kill the black first frame. (3) SIGNATURE TRACER ARC (value-tracer) — a react-native-svg Path animated via strokeDashoffset bound to a reanimated progress sharedValue (withTiming 1400ms, cubic-out), ball sprite position interpolated along the path, fading dotted trail via staggered withDelay; Haptics.impactAsync(Medium) at apex + Light at landing. Reuse the branch's existing in-app GPS tracer renderer on a demo path so onboarding == product. (4) COUNT-UP STATS (value-autocut, proof) — useSharedValue + useAnimatedProps writing into a TextInput value, eased withTiming, light haptic tick at milestones. (5) STAR-FILL SOCIAL PROOF (proof-wall, paywall) — 5 stars fill left-to-right with staggered withDelay springs (scale 1→1.2→1 overshoot) reading as 'earned'; one-time Lottie star-burst on the headline rating only. (6) CARD PARALLAX DECK (value-autocut, proof carousel) — useAnimatedScrollHandler + interpolate(scrollX) for translate/scale/rotate depth; proof carousel auto-advances every ~3s via a looping withTiming on scrollX but stays swipeable. (7) LOTTIE MICRO-ANIMATIONS — engine loader loop, sequential checkmarks, share-reaction confetti, and the trial-confirm success burst (lottie-react-native). (8) TIMELINE DRAW (paywall) — trial-timeline connecting line via strokeDashoffset, nodes pop in with withSpring; annual gold badge shimmer via a reanimated translateX gradient sweep. (9) CHIP COMMITS (q-handicap, q-goal) — withSpring entrance, interpolateColor border to #4CAF50 on select, unselected dim via withTiming; Haptics.impactAsync(Light) per tap. Confirmed: zero new dependencies, no Remotion in the live path (Remotion is offline-only for pre-rendering hero loops on the build machine, then streamed via expo-video).

### 2. [micro-commit] q-handicap
- **Headline:** What's your game like?
- **Sub:** So we tune swing detection and the tracer to your tempo.
- **Visual:** App-native: four big tap-chips stacked on the #0A0A0F bg with a faint looping tracer-arc line drawn faintly behind them. Chips: 'Just hit and hope', 'Beginner (25+)', 'Mid (15–25)', 'Single figures'.

- **Haptics:** Haptics.impactAsync(Light) on each chip tap; the selected state is the commitment so it gets the slightly firmer tick.
- **CTA:** Auto-advance ~250ms after a chip is tapped (no separate Continue button needed). Tiny 'Skip' top-right for the impatient.
- **Notes:** Store the answer in OnboardingContext. This single tap is the sunk-cost hook. Two dots of a 2-step progress indicator top-center. 'Just hit and hope' is the self-deprecating Aussie option that signals the casual audience this app is for them.

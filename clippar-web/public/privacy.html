Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 June 2026

⚠ Draft — not yet legally reviewed

This is a working draft prepared to describe how Clippar handles data. It has not been reviewed or approved by a lawyer. It must be checked against current privacy law (including the Australian Privacy Act, GDPR, and Apple's App Store requirements) before launch. Do not rely on it as a final, legally vetted policy.

This Privacy Policy explains how Clippar (“Clippar”, “we”, “us”) collects, uses, shares, and protects information when you use the Clippar mobile app and related services (the “Service”). By using the Service you agree to the practices described here.

1. Who we are

Clippar is a golf highlight-reel app that records your shots and automatically assembles them into a shareable video. You can contact us at any time at clippargolf@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your golf footage to train third-party advertising models.

3. How your video and location are handled

Video and audio are recorded only when you actively start a recording. Footage is uploaded to our processing infrastructure to detect, trim, and assemble your shots into a reel, and is stored so you can play it back, edit it, and share it. Location is used in the background only while a round is active, to associate shots with holes; you can disable location permission in your device settings, though hole-matching will be less accurate without it.

4. Third-party services we use

We share limited data with service providers who process it on our behalf, each under their own privacy terms:

  • Supabase — account authentication, database, and file storage.
  • Google and Apple — “Sign in with Google” and “Sign in with Apple” authentication.
  • PostHog — product analytics (how features are used).
  • Sentry — crash reporting and error diagnostics.
  • Stripe — payment processing for physical kit and merchandise. Card details are handled by Stripe; we do not store full card numbers.
  • Apple — in-app subscription purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store; we receive your subscription status, not your payment details.

5. Legal bases & your choices

We process your data to provide the Service you request, to comply with legal obligations, and for our legitimate interest in improving and securing the app. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data, and to object to or restrict certain processing. To exercise any of these, email clippargolf@gmail.com.

6. Data retention

We keep your account data and content for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, we delete or anonymise your personal data and recorded content within a reasonable period, except where we are required to retain certain records (for example, transaction records for tax or legal compliance). Backups containing your data are purged on a rolling schedule.

7. Deleting your account

You can delete your account and associated data at any time from Profile → Delete Account in the app. This removes your account, rounds, and recorded content from our active systems. If you have trouble, email clippargolf@gmail.com and we will action the deletion for you.

8. Children

Clippar is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Security

We use industry-standard measures — including encrypted transport (HTTPS) and access controls on our database and storage — to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. International transfers

Our service providers may process and store data in countries other than where you live. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards for these transfers.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we make material changes, we will update the “Last updated” date above and, where appropriate, notify you in the app.

12. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data? Email clippargolf@gmail.com.

1. Who we are & how to contact us

Clippar is a golf highlight-reel app: you record your shots on the course and the app automatically detects, trims, and assembles them into a shareable video. For the purposes of data-protection law, Clippar is the data controller for the personal information described here. You can contact us about privacy at any time at clippargolf@gmail.com.

3. Video, audio & location

Video and audio are recorded only when you actively start a recording in the app. Your footage is uploaded to our storage and to our video-processing service so it can be analysed, trimmed, and stitched into a reel, and is kept so you can play it back, edit it, and share it. If you create a public share link for a round, anyone with that link can view that reel until you remove the link.

Precise location is collected in the foreground only while a round is active, to associate each shot with a hole and to reconstruct ball flight. You can decline or revoke the location permission in your device settings; hole-matching and shot-tracer features will be less accurate or unavailable without it. We do not track your location in the background or when you are not recording.

6. App Store data disclosure summary

This mirrors the “App Privacy” information shown on our App Store listing. Clippar collects the following data, which is linked to your identity:

Clippar does not use any data to track you across apps or websites owned by other companies, and contains no advertising. We do not use the App Tracking Transparency identifier (IDFA).

7. Legal bases for processing (GDPR)

Where the GDPR applies, we rely on these legal bases:

  • Performance of a contract — to create your account and provide the Service (storing and serving your reels, running your subscription, fulfilling orders);
  • Consent — for camera, microphone, and precise-location access, which you grant through the iOS permission prompts and can withdraw at any time in your device settings; and for joining the website waitlist;
  • Legitimate interests — to keep the app stable and secure and to fix bugs (crash diagnostics), where those interests are not overridden by your rights.

8. Data retention

We keep your account data and content for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, we delete or disconnect your personal data and recorded content as described below. We may retain certain records where required for legal, tax, or fraud-prevention reasons — for example, transaction and shipping records associated with a merchandise order are kept as part of our order history. Backups containing your data are overwritten on a rolling cycle.

9. Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time from within the app at Profile → Delete Account. This is a real deletion, not a deactivation. When you delete your account, we:

  • delete your uploaded clips and highlight reels from storage;
  • delete your rounds, scores, shots, processing jobs, saved course presets, and usage records;
  • delete your profile and sign-in account;
  • ask RevenueCat to delete your subscriber record and revoke your “Sign in with Apple” token (best effort).

Please note: deleting your account does not cancel an active Apple subscription — you must cancel that in your iPhone's Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions. Merchandise order and shipping records are retained as part of our order history. If you created a public share link, remove it before deleting if you no longer want that reel to be viewable. If you have trouble deleting your account, email clippargolf@gmail.com and we will action it for you.

Do I need the kit to use Clippar?
No — the app works fine handheld, or with any tripod you've already got. The kit is for going hands-free: mount, clicker and charger sorted in one box, so you can film the whole round without touching your phone.
Which phones does it fit?
The mount grips standard-sized smartphones, with or without a case. The Clippar app itself is iOS, so you'll want an iPhone to get the full experience.
Will it fit my bag or buggy?
The clamp is designed for common golf bag frames, buggy handles and cart rails. If your setup is unusual, email us at clippargolf@gmail.com before you order and we'll give you a straight answer.
How does my phone last all 18 holes?
Recording video for a full round is hard on a battery, which is exactly why the kit includes a portable charger — top your phone up as you play. Battery life varies phone to phone, but the combo is built to comfortably see out 18 holes.
Do you ship Australia-wide?
Yep — we ship anywhere in Australia.
What about the app?
The kit works hand-in-glove with the Clippar iOS app, which is landing on the App Store soon. Grab the kit now and you'll be ready to film from day one — join the waitlist to hear the moment it's live.
